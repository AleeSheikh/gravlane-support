GRAVLANE Publisher: Privacy policy

GRAVLANE Publisher is a private tool used by one person: the developer of the free game GRAVLANE. It sends the developer's own game videos to the developer's own TikTok account. This page explains what data it handles. The game's own privacy policy is separate.

Who uses it

Only the developer, signed in to their own TikTok account (@gravlane_srr). It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What it receives from TikTok

  • user.info.basic: the account's display name, used to show which account is connected.
  • video.upload and video.publish: permission to send the developer's own videos to their TikTok inbox as drafts, or to post them to their own account.
  • An access and refresh token for that one account, so the connection survives between uses.

What it stores

Only the encrypted login token and the account's display name, in a private database that only the developer can open. It does not store, copy or analyse anything from other TikTok users, and it does not read the developer's followers, messages or watch history.

What it does not do

It never sells, shares or discloses TikTok data. It does not use TikTok data to train any model, does not track anyone, and does not post anything without the developer pressing the post button themselves.

Videos

The videos it sends are cut from the game's own gameplay footage, made by the developer, and hosted on this site. They contain no other person's content.

Deleting your data

The developer can disconnect at any time from TikTok's app settings (Security and permissions → Manage app permissions) and the stored token is then useless. To have the stored token and display name erased, email gravlane.support@gmail.com.
