GRAVLANE Publisher: Terms of service

GRAVLANE Publisher is a private, single-user tool made by the developer of the free game GRAVLANE. These terms describe how it may be used.

1. What it is

A personal tool that sends the developer's own gameplay videos to the developer's own TikTok account, either as inbox drafts or as posts the developer chooses to publish. It is not a public service and is not available to other people.

2. Use

It may be used only by the developer, only on their own TikTok account, and only with videos the developer made. It must be used in line with TikTok's Terms of Service, Community Guidelines, Developer Terms and Content Sharing Guidelines. Every post is started by the developer pressing a button; nothing is posted by itself.

3. What it must not be used for

Spam, mass posting, fake engagement, posting other people's content, misleading or unlabelled promotional content, or anything else TikTok's rules forbid.

4. Data

See the privacy policy. In short: only a login token and the account's display name are stored, and nothing is shared.

5. No warranty

The tool is provided as it is. TikTok may change or withdraw its API at any time, and the tool may stop working when it does.

6. Changes

If these terms change, the date below changes with them.
