Delete your account

published by  Burgerizza 

This page permanently deletes your GRAVLANE account and everything stored with it. You can do the same thing inside the game under Settings → Pilot → Delete account. Both routes do exactly the same work.

What is deleted

  • Your account and its Google sign-in link
  • Your callsign, and the claim on that name
  • Every level you published, including its plays and ratings
  • Every rating you gave and every level completion recorded for you
  • Your cloud save — progress, best times, unlocks and statistics

This cannot be undone. Levels you published are removed for everyone, not hidden.

What is kept

Nothing. No copy of your data is retained after deletion and none of it is sold or shared with anyone. Encrypted database backups may still hold a copy for up to 7 days, after which they roll off and it is gone permanently.

Playing without an account

GRAVLANE can be played offline with no account at all. Guest progress is stored only on your own device, is never uploaded, and cannot be identified from this page. To remove it, use Settings → Gameplay → Erase everything in the game, or uninstall the app.
